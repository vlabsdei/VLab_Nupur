### Procedure
1. Open the Thermal Oxidation Virtual Lab simulation.

2. Select the oxidation type:
   - Dry Oxidation
   - Wet Oxidation

3. Set the process temperature using the slider.
   - Allowed range: **900°C – 1200°C**

4. Set the heating time using the slider.
   - Allowed range: **0 – 180 minutes**

5. Observe the furnace setup, gas inlet, and silicon wafer inside the oxidation chamber.

6. Click the **Start Oxidation** button to begin the process.

7. Observe the following changes during oxidation:
   - Gas particles entering the furnace
   - Furnace heating effect
   - Growth of the SiO₂ layer on the silicon wafer

8. Study the output parameters displayed:
   - Oxide Thickness
   - Growth Rate
   - Layer Quality
   - Furnace Status

9. Compare the oxide growth behavior for:
   - Dry oxidation
   - Wet oxidation

10. Observe how increasing:
    - Temperature
    - Heating time

    affects oxide thickness and growth rate.

11. Click the **Reset** button to restart the experiment with new parameters.

12. Analyze the results using the Deal-Grove oxidation model and compare the oxide layer quality under different operating conditions.
