### About the Experiment

The manufacturing of semiconductor chips begins with the production of a high-purity silicon wafer. And once this wafer is safely manufactured, this wafer goes through an oxidation process wherein a layer of SiO2 is built over it. This keeps it insulated and protected.
There are two types of oxdiation processes - Dry oxidation and Wet Oxidation
Through this experiment, students can simulate both the oxidation types along with other parametric changes such as Temperature and Time and get the output in terms of the oxide thickness and growth rate.
