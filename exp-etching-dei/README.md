### About the Experiment
This Virtual Lab simulates the **Semiconductor Etching Process**, a critical step in integrated circuit fabrication where unwanted material is selectively removed from a patterned silicon wafer.

The experiment allows students to explore both **Wet Chemical Etching** and **Dry Reactive Ion Etching (RIE)** by adjusting key process parameters such as chemical concentration, RF plasma power, and etching time. Through interactive visualization, users can observe material removal, compare isotropic and anisotropic etch profiles, analyze etch rates, and evaluate process quality.

### Learning Objectives
- Understand the role of etching in semiconductor manufacturing.
- Compare wet etching and dry plasma etching techniques.
- Study the effect of process parameters on etch rate and pattern quality.
- Observe isotropic and anisotropic etch profiles.
- Analyze the relationship between process conditions and fabrication yield.
