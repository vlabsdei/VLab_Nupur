### About the Experiment

The manufacturing of semiconductor chips relies on establishing high-conductivity pathways to interlink internal transistors. Once these microscopic components are isolated and tested, the wafer goes through a metal wiring (metallization) process wherein a thin, conductive film is deposited over it and patterned to create active circuit interconnects.There are two primary types of deposition techniques used for wiring: Chemical Vapor Deposition (CVD), which provides highly conformal and uniform step coverage, and Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD), which uses sputtering kinetics for directional metallization paths.Through this experiment, students can simulate both deposition methods using varying materials such as Aluminum (Al), Copper (Cu), or Silicon Dioxide (SiO_2), along with other parametric changes such as Deposition Rate and Time, obtaining live readouts of the final film thickness, step coverage profiles, and overall layer uniformity.
