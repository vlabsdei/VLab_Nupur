### Procedure
1. Click the **Silicon Wafer** to place it into the Spin Coater.

2. Click the **Photoresist (PR)** bottle to drop the liquid polymer onto the wafer.

3. Click **Spin Coat**. The machine spins the wafer to spread the liquid into a thin, even layer.

4. Click **Soft Bake**. The hotplate heats the wafer to dry the liquid resist and make it stick.

5. Click the **Photomask** to place your circuit pattern directly over the wafer.

6. Click **UV Exposure**. Ultraviolet light shines through the mask to change the chemical structure of the exposed resist.

7. Click **Develop**. The wafer enters a chemical bath that washes away the exposed areas, leaving behind your circuit lines.

8. Click **Hard Bake**. A final high-temperature bake hardens the remaining pattern so it is ready for manufacturing.
